Chain of custody

Proof at every single touchpoint.

A delivery isn't just an address. It's a record. From the moment we take possession to the moment a signature is captured, Swift documents who held it, where it was, and what it looked like.

Admissible documentation

Our custody logs have been accepted as supporting evidence in civil proceedings. Each record carries timestamps, courier identity, and photographic proof.

See the full custody process
01

Item intake & condition record

We photograph the item, weigh it, log any existing marks, and record the sender's ID before anything leaves the room.

02

Tamper-evident sealing

Every envelope and case is closed with a numbered seal. The number is registered on the waybill and never reused.

03

Witnessed handoff

Transition of possession happens in front of a witness or on recorded bodycam, with both parties confirming the seal number.

04

Encrypted in-transit monitoring

GPS pings every 30 seconds, with automatic alerts if the courier deviates from the planned corridor by more than 400 metres.

05

Signature, seal check & archive

The recipient verifies the seal is unbroken, signs digitally, and the complete record is archived for seven years.
