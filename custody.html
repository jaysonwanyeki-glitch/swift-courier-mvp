Chain of custody

Proof at every single touchpoint.

A delivery isn't just an address. It's a record. From the moment we take possession to the moment a signature is captured, Swift documents who held it, where it was, and what it looked like.

01

Item intake & condition record

We photograph the item, weigh it, log any existing marks, and record the sender's ID before anything leaves the room.

02

Tamper-evident sealing

Every envelope and case is closed with a numbered seal. The number is registered on the waybill and never reused.

03

Witnessed handoff

Transition of possession happens in front of a witness or on recorded bodycam, with both parties confirming the seal number.

04

Encrypted in-transit monitoring

GPS pings every 30 seconds, with automatic alerts if the courier deviates from the planned corridor by more than 400 metres.

05

Signature, seal check & archive

The recipient verifies the seal is unbroken, signs digitally, and the complete record is archived for seven years.

7 yrs

Record retention

Every run's custody record is retained for seven years and can be produced on request, quoted by reference number.

Admissibility

Built to survive a courtroom.

Our custody logs have been accepted as supporting evidence in civil proceedings. Each record carries timestamps, courier identity, seal numbers and photographic proof — signed at the point of capture so it cannot be edited after the fact.

Cryptographic signing

The log is hashed and signed at the moment of capture, so any later alteration is detectable.

Timestamped transitions

Every change of possession is recorded with a timestamp, the courier's identity, and the seal number in force at that moment.

Printable copies

Any custody log can be printed or saved as PDF straight from the tracking page — useful for filing alongside your own records.

Couriers

Who is allowed to carry it.

Background screening, fingerprinting, and a five-year employment check — repeated annually. Couriers assigned to confidential records or legal matters sign an individual NDA and are photographed on every run. We never use contractors or gig drivers on Swift-branded routes.
